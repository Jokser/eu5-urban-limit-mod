[h1]Urban Limit Reworked[/h1]

In vanilla, every location can sooner or later become a town or a city, and by the late game whole countries are covered in them. This mod keeps urbanization limited and makes it follow geography: towns and cities grow where climate, water and local resources could historically support them, and deserts, tundra and bare mountains stay rural.

It is a rework of [b]Urban limit[/b] by [b]Emperor VI[/b] (part of his Total Realism Mod). The idea and the province caps come from the original: cities do not grow everywhere, only where conditions allow them. The geography rules are rewritten.

[h2]What changed compared with Urban limit[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]One clear rule per climate[/b] instead of separate terrain, vegetation and climate gates. Each of the game's climates has its own conditions for a town and for a city.
[*][b]Water matters:[/b] rivers, harbours and lake shores let towns and cities grow in places that would otherwise be too harsh, as the river valleys and ports of history did.
[*][b]Local resources matter:[/b] farming land feeds market towns, and trade goods such as silk, saffron, incense or silver build cities even in hard country.
[*][b]Tooltips explain the rule[/b] for the location's climate when an upgrade is not allowed.
[*][b]The location rank file follows the current game version[/b], so no vanilla rank bonuses are lost.
[/list]

[h2]Province caps (from the original)[/h2]
[list]
[*]A province with 1-3 locations can have one urban centre: a city or a town.
[*]A province with 4-5 locations can have one city and one town.
[*]A province with 6 or more locations can have one city and up to two towns.
[/list]
Large empires cannot turn every location into a city.

[h2]Geography: one rule per climate[/h2]
Water means a river, a harbour of at least 0.25 or a lake shore. "Any water" also counts a plain sea coast.
[table]
[tr][th]Climate[/th][th]Town[/th][th]City[/th][/tr]
[tr][td]Arctic[/td][td]not in mountains, with a good harbour[/td][td]never[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Subpolar[/td][td]not in mountains, with water[/td][td]flat land, plateau or wetlands, with a river, a good harbour or a lake[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Continental, semi-arid[/td][td]anywhere but bare mountains; mountains need water[/td][td]farmland or grassland, or water[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Mediterranean, oceanic, subtropical[/td][td]anywhere[/td][td]not in mountains, or mountains with water[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Arid, cold arid[/td][td]any water, or farmland outside mountains[/td][td]not in mountains, with any water or farmland[/td][/tr]
[tr][td]Tropical[/td][td]not in mountains, with any water, farmland or grassland, or open hills and plateaus[/td][td]not in mountains, with any water, farmland or grassland[/td][/tr]
[/table]

Two exceptions on top of the table:
[list]
[*]A [b]food resource[/b] (wheat, rice, fruit, millet, legumes, maize, potato) allows a town outside mountains and the arctic: a fertile oasis or river valley feeds a market town.
[*]A [b]valuable resource[/b] (silk, saffron, cotton, gems, silver, gold, incense) allows a town and a city (no city in the arctic): trade wealth builds cities even in hard country.
[/list]
Checked against the 1337 start: 36 of the 1067 historical towns and cities fail the rules, mostly oases and desert trade cities whose rivers or wells are not on the map. They keep their rank; the rules only decide new upgrades.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Do not use together with the original Urban limit.[/b] They share the same triggers.
[*]Replaces [i]in_game/common/location_ranks/00_default.txt[/i] whole. Mods that change the same file will conflict.
[*]Save compatible: it can be added to a running game. The rules only decide upgrades, so existing towns and cities keep their rank.
[/list]
